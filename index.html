About Me

My instinct is to question assumptions. When everyone else is thinking about what should happen, I'm thinking about what could go wrong — and documenting it clearly enough that it actually gets fixed.

I work through the QA process end-to-end: analyzing requirements, designing structured test cases, executing manual and exploratory testing, and logging defects with full reproducibility so developers can act on them immediately.

Through Leapfrog Connect's QA Foundations training, I've practiced this process on real platforms — including API testing with Postman and UI automation concepts with Cypress.

As a CS student I also understand how software is constructed, which makes me a sharper tester — I know where the likely failure points are before I even open the app.
